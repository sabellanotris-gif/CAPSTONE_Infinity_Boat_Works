Delivery Terms (what the customer signs)

    The customer signs these terms in their project page once the boat is In Transit or Delivered. Signing also marks the delivery as confirmed.

    Delay Penalty Rule

    • 1% of boat price — 1 to 7 days past committed date
    • 2% of boat price — 8 to 14 days past committed date
    • 5% of boat price — 15 to 30 days past committed date
    • 8% of boat price — more than 30 days past committed date

    Penalties are deducted from the remaining balance and are computed automatically from the committed and actual delivery dates.
